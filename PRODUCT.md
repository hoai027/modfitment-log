# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Node with built-in SQLite and vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no dependencies, so it runs locally with one command.

## Users

One car enthusiast working on their own car, on a phone or laptop in a garage, sometimes with dirty hands or poor light.

## Product Purpose

Record what is planned, installed or removed on a car, and show how a wheel and tire setup changes fitment compared with a baseline before parts are bought.

## Positioning

Separates installed from planned parts, and proof from forum rumor, on every entry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single user and local. No accounts. Fitment results are geometry only: camber, suspension travel and ride height are not modeled, and the app contains no vehicle specifications that the user did not enter.

## Evidence on Hand

No customers or benchmarks exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

- Never present a guess as a confirmed fit.
- Say which way a part moved in words, not only with a sign.
- Warnings name the problem and what to check next.
